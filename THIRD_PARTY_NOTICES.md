## AutoEq

`Sources/HankoMix/Resources/autoeq.json` is generated by `scripts/generate-autoeq.sh` from the
parametric EQ results in [AutoEq](https://github.com/jaakkopasanen/AutoEq) at commit
`7ae0f56d53074872b028649617a22bbb4232feb7`. The measurements behind those results come from the
sources named in each preset's `source` field (oratory1990, crinacle, Rtings, Innerfidelity and others).
